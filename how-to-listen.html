How to listen

← Back to EarthTunes

Start

  • Pick a dataset.
  • Press Play (or K).
  • Headphones help: sounds move left and right.

What you hear

  • Higher notes = more: warmer water, brighter lights, more lightning, more CO₂ in view.
  • Left or right = where on the map.
  • Audio legend (G) plays the scale.

Explore

  • Press E or Explore, then move with the arrow keys.
  • W = where am I, C = compare, Esc = back.

More

  • The Live description under the video says what you hear in words.
  • Press ? or for all keys.
  • B hides the dataset list.

Data and visualizations: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, shown at the NASA Earth Information Center. · Recorded instruments: VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0). · Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
